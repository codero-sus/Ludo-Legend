    📖 How to Play

    1. 🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base onto the board.
    2. 👆 After rolling, tap a glowing token to move it. Tokens move clockwise around the board.
    3. ⭐ Star squares are safe — tokens there can't be captured. All start squares are safe too.
    4. ⚔️ Land on an enemy token on a non-safe square to capture it — it goes back to base! You earn a bonus roll.
    5. 🎁 Rolling a 6 or bringing a token home grants an extra turn.
    6. 🚫 Roll three 6s in a row and your turn is skipped (no move on the third 6).
    7. 🏠 Enter the coloured home column and reach the centre with an exact roll.
    8. 🏆 First player to bring all 4 tokens home wins the game!
